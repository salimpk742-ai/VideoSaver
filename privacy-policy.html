Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At VideoSaver, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the VideoSaver website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

VideoSaver is designed to operate with minimal collection of personal information. Depending on how the website is used, we may receive information such as:

  • Information you voluntarily provide through a contact form or email.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type and operating system.
  • IP address and general usage information that may be provided automatically by web servers or security services.
  • Information about how visitors interact with the website.

We do not ask users to create an account simply to use the basic website.

2. Video URLs

When you submit a video URL to VideoSaver, that URL may be processed by the service in order to provide the requested functionality.

Users should not submit private, confidential or sensitive information through the VideoSaver service.

VideoSaver is not intended to permanently store users' videos. Processing and retention may depend on the technical systems used to operate the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Providing and improving the service.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud or security problems.
  • Understanding website usage and performance.
  • Responding to user questions and support requests.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies

VideoSaver may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, maintain and improve the website.

Cookies may also be used by third-party services integrated into the website, such as analytics, security or advertising providers.

You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Advertising

VideoSaver may display advertisements in the future. Advertising partners may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements.

Where required, users may be provided with choices regarding personalized advertising and cookies.

Advertising services and partners may change over time. When advertising is enabled on VideoSaver, this Privacy Policy may be updated to identify relevant services and provide additional information where required.

6. Analytics

VideoSaver may use analytics services to understand website traffic, performance and user interaction.

Analytics providers may collect technical information such as device information, browser information, approximate location and pages visited.

Analytics services will only be used where appropriate and may be subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Services

VideoSaver may rely on third-party services for hosting, security, analytics, advertising, content processing or other website functionality.

These third parties may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

VideoSaver is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

10. External Websites

VideoSaver may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain processing.

You may also control cookies through your browser and, where applicable, advertising or consent settings provided on the website.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of VideoSaver, please contact us through the Contact page.
